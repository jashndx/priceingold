What is this tool?

This research tool prices Indian stocks in pure physical 24K Gold instead of nominal Indian Rupees (₹).

1
The Fiat Illusion

In Rupees, almost every stock looks like it's going up over time simply because more money is printed and the currency loses purchasing power.

2
True Purchasing Power (mg/share)

Gold cannot be inflated away. Measuring 1 share in milligrams of Gold reveals whether a company actually beat currency debasement or just tagged along with inflation.

3
Two Simple Graph Views

Toggle between mg/share (Gold valuation) and Real INR (Rupee price deflated by Gold to show real purchasing power).
